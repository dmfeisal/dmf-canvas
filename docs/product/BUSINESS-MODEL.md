# Business model hypotheses

No Canvas revenue, customer demand, pricing or hosting economics has been measured. Public source licensing remains open; self-hostable direction does not decide an OSS license.

Propose a local/free creator path and optional paid hosted/private projects, version history and privacy-conscious analytics. Team review/collaboration and commercial SDK licensing are later experiments. The supplied [benchmark](../research/COMPETITOR-BENCHMARK.md) includes hypothetical price ranges; these are not Canvas offers, approved prices or independently refreshed competitor prices.

Test willingness to pay in creator sessions, compare build/self-host effort with hosting convenience and measure storage, asset delivery, bandwidth and support costs before selecting tiers. Avoid runtime royalties that discourage public distribution unless evidence changes the strategy. Alternative: entirely self-hosted tooling with paid support; demand is also unknown.

Dependencies: independent artifact ownership, reliable publishing, license review and privacy decisions. Billing implementation and enterprise promises are outside MVP. Product owner approves any commercial experiment after [validation](VALIDATION-PLAN.md).
