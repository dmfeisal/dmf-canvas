# Competitive landscape

The [25-product benchmark](COMPETITOR-BENCHMARK.md) is a supplied structured extraction of a larger report, not a fresh independent audit. Its claims, prices and confidence labels retain the original author's provenance; source verification remains pending.

Working hypothesis: Canvas can differentiate around creator-owned, imported visual thinking published as an interactive artifact on an existing website. Excalidraw/tldraw are important import/infrastructure alternatives; Figma/Miro own upstream workflows; Vev/Readymag and site builders compete on publishing; Onlook/Builder.io matter to the later frontend track. These are strategic interpretations, not measured opportunity scores.

Do not duplicate financial tables here or treat company usage as product usage. Before external or pricing decisions, recheck official sources and record verification date, billing basis, measurement year and unit. Missing data remains Undisclosed or Not tested. Roadmap implications are proposals in the [product roadmap](../product/PRODUCT-ROADMAP.md).
