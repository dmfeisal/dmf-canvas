# Creator interview plan

No interviews or findings are recorded. Hypotheses: creators have reusable boards, static screenshots lose useful explanation, independent embeds are preferable to share links, and setup effort is acceptable.

Recruit through owned creator communities with consent. Ask for the last real case study, how it reached the site, why existing embeds were accepted/rejected, and what had to be redrawn. Observe artifacts before describing Canvas. Ask about privacy/self-host needs and willingness to maintain embeds; avoid implying an approved price.

Record participant code, consent, context, date, artifact permission, observations/quotes separately from interpretation, and confidence changes. Never publish personal or proprietary board content without authorization. Product owns synthesis and [validation gates](../product/VALIDATION-PLAN.md).
